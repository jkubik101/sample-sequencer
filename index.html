Aarhus Improvisational Rhythm Sequencer (AIRS)

Your job Combine short rhythms into longer sequences in an improvisational way. Don't worry about a final product, just have fun generating sequences that you enjoy in the moment!

How it works The sequencer is set up as a grid where each row of the grid corresponds to a different short rhythm. As the sequencer moves from left to right across the 4 columns, it will play whichever rhythm you have selected by clicking the corresponding square.

The rhythms are organized into 3 sets, representing 3 different drummers with 7 'signature' rhythms each. You can navigate between sets as the sequence plays but it won't switch between sets until it reaches the end (last column) of the current set.

If you need a reminder of these instructions while sequencing, click on the question mark in the top right

First we will get you to listen to each rhythm so that you know what you are working with. Click 'Explore the sets' to have a listen.

Before you begin

Explore the Sets

Please listen to all the rhythms before proceeding — you must hear every sample in each set before you can get started. You can listen to a full set in sequence, or explore individual rhythms one by one. A set will be marked as heard once all its rhythms have been played through to completion.
